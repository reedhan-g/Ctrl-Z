## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Ctrl Z is a browser-based isometric infrastructure simulation set in Meridian. Remove electricity, observe dependent services, intervene with limited supplies, and rewind to compare outcomes.

## Users

The creator wants a polished, accessible presentation for the Embarko Student Challenge. The homepage helps a new visitor understand the project and enter the existing playable simulation. Other audience segments remain unspecified.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing game includes Sandbox and Investigation, six service identities, contextual inspection, dependency tracing, supplies, replay/undo, reports, local saves, minimap navigation and mobile controls. The landing-page task must preserve the entire game UI, city renderer, artwork, controls, simulation, persistence and deployment workflow.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Ctrl Z. Tagline: Undo the World. The game retains the approved neon isometric city, Antahonist Neon City reference direction and commit-27 gameplay lineage; rejected commit-28/29 designs remain excluded. The homepage uses the creator's explicit minimal editorial, dark, restrained-neon direction.

## Evidence on Hand

Current game captures in docs/ui-overhaul/deployed and docs/ui-overhaul/final document the implemented interface at main 0ac6f5a. Use actual gameplay imagery. Do not invent testimonials, usage metrics, awards or unimplemented scenarios.

## Product Principles

- The playable city is the product; presentation should make its mechanism clear.
- Preserve tested behavior while introducing independently recoverable changes.
- Support keyboard, desktop and mobile web use.
- Keep the homepage lightweight and clearly distinguish previews from live play.

## Game Presentation

The creator chose to embed the game below the landing page. Load it on demand in an isolated same-origin frame, with a full-screen option. No other audience or commercial claims have been supplied.
